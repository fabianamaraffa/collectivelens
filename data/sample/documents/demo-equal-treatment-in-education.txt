Human rights do not stop at the school gate. This report by Demo Rights Forum collects evidence on discrimination in schools and universities across Europe and sets out what institutions must do to make education equal and inclusive.

Students report discrimination on many grounds: ethnicity, religion, disability, gender and sexual orientation, and migration background. LGBTIQ+ students in particular describe harassment and a lack of recognition of their identity in forms, in classrooms and in student housing. Students with disabilities describe buildings, exams and digital tools that were not designed with them in mind. In many cases the person who experienced discrimination does not report it, because they do not know where to go or fear consequences.

We recommend binding equality and diversity plans for every education institution, with measurable targets and public reporting. Staff should receive regular training on equality and non-discrimination. Each institution needs a safe and independent channel for complaints, with clear deadlines and protection against retaliation. Accessibility should be a condition for public funding, not an optional extra.

Inclusion is also a matter of curriculum. History, literature and civics should reflect the diversity of society, and teaching materials should be reviewed for stereotypes. Student organisations that represent minority groups should receive funding and a seat in decision-making bodies.

Equality is a legal duty and a democratic value. Education that excludes some students fails all of them.
